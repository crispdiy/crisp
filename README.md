## What is Crisp?

**Crisp is a website that builds apps and websites for you, like having your own developer. Say what you want, in your own words, and Crisp makes it, page by page, while you watch. No coding, no hiring, no waiting.**

Crisp is an AI app builder. Anyone can build apps and websites now, not just developers. Say what you want in plain words, and Crisp builds the whole app or website, with every page, the data it saves and sign-in for your users. That is the DIY in Crisp. You do it yourself, with no developer, no team and no budget.

Crisp lives at [crisp.diy](https://crisp.diy). It runs in the browser, and there is nothing to install.

Not the Crisp you were looking for? [Crisp](https://crisp.chat) (crisp.chat) is an unrelated customer messaging and live chat product. [CRISPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRISPR) is a gene editing method. This repository is about Crisp the AI app builder at crisp.diy.

## What does DIY mean in Crisp?

DIY means do it yourself. You build your own app or website, the way you would put together furniture or paint a room, without hiring anyone. Crisp does the technical work and you make the decisions.

It does not mean self-hosted. In [bolt.diy](https://bolt.diy) the name means you run the tool on your own computer. In Crisp the name means you, a person without a developer, make the thing you need. Crisp is a hosted product at crisp.diy, and this repository is not a copy of it you can run.

## What is Crisp AI?

Crisp AI is the part of Crisp that reads your words and turns them into a working app. You describe who uses the app, what they do and how it should feel. Crisp AI writes every page, sets up the data the app saves, adds sign-in for your users and shows you the result as it is made.

You never see a prompt engineering screen or a model picker. You type the way you would explain the idea to a friend. When something is not right, you say so, and Crisp AI fixes it. You never pay for Crisp fixing its own mistakes.

The apps you build can use AI too. The built in [AI connector](https://crisp.diy/connectors/crisp-ai) gives your app chat helpers, summaries, classification, extraction and writing tools with Claude models, called through Crisp, with no API key to paste.

## How Crisp works

1. **Describe it.** Write what you want the way you would explain it to a friend. Who uses it, what they do, how it should feel.
2. **Watch it come together.** Every screen, every saved record, every button that works appears as it is made. Nothing to set up, nothing to install.
3. **Try it as it is built.** Your app is live from the first minute. Open it, click around, break it, ask for the fix. No waiting for a build.
4. **Change anything.** Make the menu stick to the top. Let people sign in with Google. Mention the dress code in the confirmation email. Every change is one sentence.
5. **Undo any step, data included.** Go back to any earlier reply in one click, and the data goes back with it.
6. **Publish when it is ready.** Your app gets a permanent address, and you can connect your own domain.

## What can I make with Crisp?

Most useful software is small. A booking page for one studio. A tracker for one team. A tool that does one job the way you like it. These apps were rarely built, because building them needed a developer and a budget. Crisp is for exactly these apps.

- **Websites.** Sites with forms, bookings and sign-in. [AI website builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-website-builder)
- **Mobile apps.** Real iPhone and Android apps. [AI mobile app builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-mobile-app-builder)
- **Booking apps.** Classes, appointments and time slots. [AI booking app builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-booking-app-builder)
- **CRMs.** Contacts, a pipeline and tasks. [AI CRM builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-crm-builder)
- **Dashboards.** Internal tools, tables and charts. [AI dashboard builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-dashboard-builder)
- **SaaS products.** Accounts, plans and payments. [AI SaaS builder](https://crisp.diy/build/ai-saas-builder)

## Who is Crisp for?

Crisp is for [small businesses](https://crisp.diy/for/small-businesses), [agencies and freelancers](https://crisp.diy/for/agencies-and-freelancers), [startups](https://crisp.diy/for/startups) and [students](https://crisp.diy/for/students). It is for anyone who has needed an app and did not have a developer.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. You write in plain words. If you do know how to code, the code is all readable, editable and yours. Change a line yourself and keep chatting, or export the whole thing to GitHub.

## Your code and data are yours

Everything Crisp builds belongs to you. Export the code to GitHub whenever you want and take it anywhere. Your users' data stays in your app. Read more on [ownership](https://crisp.diy/docs/ownership).

## What does Crisp cost?

You can start for free, and no credit card is required. The Free plan includes a monthly amount of AI usage, enough to build and try your first apps. You always see what a request costs while it runs. Every detail is on the [pricing page](https://crisp.diy/pricing).

## Crisp compared to other AI app builders

Crisp is often compared with Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, Base44 and Emergent. The difference is who Crisp is for. Those tools are mostly for people who already build software and want to go faster. Crisp is for people who have never built software and want the app anyway. The comparisons are on the [compare pages](https://crisp.diy/compare).
