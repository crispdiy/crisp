# Example prompts for Crisp

These are the kinds of things people type into [Crisp](https://crisp.diy). Write the way you would explain the idea to a friend. Say who uses it, what they do and how it should feel. Crisp asks when something is unclear.

## Websites

- A website for my bakery with the menu, opening hours, a map and a form to order a cake for a date
- A portfolio site for a photographer with galleries by category and a contact form
- A landing page for my newsletter with a sign-up box and an archive of past issues

## Bookings

- A booking page for my yoga studio where people pick a class and a time, and get a confirmation email
- Clinic appointments with a calendar for each doctor and reminders the day before
- A tool to rent out my two meeting rooms by the hour

## Teams and small businesses

- A CRM for a three person team with contacts, a pipeline and tasks
- An invoice maker where I add my clients, make invoices and mark them paid
- A tuition fee tracker for my coaching centre with students, monthly fees and who still owes

## Personal and events

- A guest list for our wedding where people pick a meal
- A habit tracker with streaks and a weekly summary
- A recipe box with search and a shopping list made from the recipes I pick

## Mobile apps

- An iPhone and Android app for my gym where members check in with a QR code
- A field app for my delivery drivers with today's stops and a button to mark each one done

## Changes you can ask for after the first build

- Make the menu stick to the top when I scroll
- Let people sign in with Google
- The confirmation email should mention the dress code
- Add a dark theme and remember which one each person picked
- Export everything to GitHub

More in the [example prompts](https://crisp.diy/docs/example-prompts) and [writing prompts](https://crisp.diy/docs/writing-prompts) docs.
